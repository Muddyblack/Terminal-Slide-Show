## 0.1.0

First release of the `muddyblack.terminal_slideshow` collection.

- Role `slideshow`: backend from a checksum-verified release, Chromium-in-cage or MPV display,
  screen power by schedule, unattended upgrades, watchdog, SSH hardening and an nftables firewall.
- Playbooks: `site`, `update`, `status`, `reboot`, `maintenance`.
